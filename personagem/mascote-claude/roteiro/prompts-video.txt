MASCOTE CLAUDE — PROMPTS PARA GERAR O VÍDEO (image-to-video)

COMO USAR
- Ferramentas de vídeo por imagem (Kling, Runway, Veo, Hailuo, Luma e similares):
  envie a imagem da cena como QUADRO INICIAL e cole o prompt da cena.
- Os prompts estão em inglês porque essas ferramentas costumam segui-los melhor.
  Cada um traz um resumo em português.
- Formato 9:16, 1080 × 1920. Se a ferramenta só gerar 5 ou 10 s, gere e corte para 6 s.
- Gere o vídeo SEM fala. A narração (roteiro-narracao.txt) é gravada ou gerada à parte
  e montada no editor junto com trilha, efeitos e legendas.
- Anexe referencia/mascote-base.png (e mascote-vistas.png, se a ferramenta aceitar
  mais de uma referência) para reforçar a consistência do personagem.
- Gere 2 a 4 versões por cena e escolha a que preserva melhor o personagem.

------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCO DE CONSISTÊNCIA (já incluído em cada prompt abaixo)
A tiny cute 3D robot mascot with a wide orange rounded-rectangle head, two square
glossy black eyes with small white highlights, faint pink cheeks, a tiny blocky smile,
black headphones with glowing orange rims, a minimalist black tunic with a belt, small
pocket and a glowing buckle, a glowing four-pointed star on top of its head, four
short orange legs, holding a light ruler with glowing orange tips in its right hand.
Small enough to walk on laptop keys. Polished 3D animated-film look, dark room,
cinematic orange lighting, shallow depth of field.

PROMPT NEGATIVO (use em todas as cenas, se a ferramenta aceitar)
character redesign, changing proportions, extra limbs, extra eyes, mouth talking,
melting, morphing, deformation, flicker, color shift, different outfit, logo, brand
text, random text, distorted screen text, warped keyboard, fast camera, shaky camera,
human hands, realistic human

------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 1 — O DESPERTAR · quadro inicial: imagens/cena-01-despertar.png · 6 s
Resumo: a estrela acende, ele abre os olhos, olha para a câmera e bate a régua numa tecla.

Prompt:
[Bloco de consistência] The mascot stands still on the laptop keyboard with its head
slightly lowered. The star on its head lights up softly, its eyes open and it blinks
once. It raises its head and looks into the camera with a curious, confident tilt.
Then it lifts the ruler and taps the glowing key in front of it; the key presses down
and pulses orange. The laptop screen behind it brightens gently from bottom to top,
rim-lighting the mascot. Very slow camera push-in. Smooth, subtle motion; the
character keeps exactly the same design.

------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 2 — ORGANIZANDO A TIMELINE · quadro inicial: imagens/cena-02-timeline.png · 6 s
Resumo: dá alguns passinhos sobre as teclas e aponta a régua para os clipes da timeline.

Prompt:
[Bloco de consistência] The mascot takes two or three small steps across the keys
toward the center, then stops, turns slightly toward the screen and raises the ruler
to point at the video clips on the editing timeline. On the screen the selected clip
keeps its orange outline and the red playhead moves slowly to the right. The mascot
glances back at the camera as if explaining. Slow lateral camera move following the
mascot, focus on the mascot. The on-screen video-editing interface stays stable and
legible; do not invent new text.

------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 3 — CRIANDO ANIMAÇÕES · quadro inicial: imagens/cena-03-animacoes.png · 6 s
Resumo: ele "rege" com a régua enquanto título, selo e legenda se animam no anúncio.

Prompt:
[Bloco de consistência] Standing at the bottom edge of the laptop screen, the mascot
moves the ruler in small conducting gestures. With each gesture an element of the
vertical product ad on screen animates: the title gently scales in, the round discount
badge slides along its dotted motion path, the caption slides up from the bottom,
small sparkles twinkle. The blue playhead moves across the keyframe timeline. The
mascot lowers the ruler and looks at the screen, pleased. Gentle camera tilt up from
the mascot to the ad. Keep the ad layout and its existing text exactly as in the image.

------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 4 — ANTES E DEPOIS · quadro inicial: imagens/cena-04-antes-depois.png · 6 s
Resumo: olha o original, depois o editado, aponta a régua para o editado e dá um pulinho.

Prompt:
[Bloco de consistência] The mascot looks at the left "Original" panel on the screen,
then turns its head to the right "Edited" panel. It points the ruler at the edited
video; the small step labels under it light up one by one. It does a tiny happy hop.
Calm, satisfied expression. Static camera with a very slow push-in. Both video panels
stay exactly as in the image; no extra text, no changes to the comparison.

------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 5 — ENCERRAMENTO · quadro inicial: imagens/cena-05-encerramento.png · 6 s
Resumo: se acomoda sentado, olha para a câmera, acena e o quadro fica estável no final.

Prompt:
[Bloco de consistência] The mascot settles into a sitting position on the keyboard,
resting the ruler against its shoulder. It looks into the camera and waves goodbye
with its left hand, two short gentle waves. The orange backlight behind it slowly
grows warmer; the star pulses softly. The top third of the frame stays dark and empty
for closing text. Slow camera pull-back. Hold the final frame steady for the last second.

------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT ÚNICO (para ferramentas que geram o vídeo inteiro a partir de várias imagens)
A 30-second vertical 9:16 animated ad in five 6-second shots, using the five attached
images as the first frame of each shot, in order. [Bloco de consistência]
Shot 1: the mascot wakes up on a laptop keyboard, looks at the camera and taps a key
with its ruler; the screen lights up. Shot 2: it walks over the keys and points the
ruler at clips on a video-editing timeline. Shot 3: it conducts with the ruler while
titles, a badge and captions animate in a vertical product ad on screen. Shot 4: it
compares the original and edited versions and points at the edited one, pleased.
Shot 5: it sits on the keyboard, waves goodbye to the camera, warm orange backlight,
dark empty space at the top for closing text. Subtle, smooth motion, identical
character in every shot, no dialogue, no lip sync, no new on-screen text.

------------------------------------------------------------
O QUE AINDA DEPENDE DE FERRAMENTA EXTERNA
- Animação das imagens: ferramenta de image-to-video (ou animação 3D a partir das cenas).
- Narração: gravação de voz ou ferramenta de texto-para-fala em português brasileiro.
- Montagem final (cortes, trilha, efeitos, legendas, mixagem e exportação): editor de vídeo.
