MASCOTE CLAUDE — PROMPTS DE ANIMAÇÃO COM MOTION
Vídeo: "Coisas que ninguém te conta sobre ser uma IA" · 38 s · 9:16 · comédia leve

Estes prompts são a base do vídeo que eu produzi em 3D (mesmo mascote, mesmas cenas).
Também servem para gerar uma versão em ferramentas de vídeo por IA (Kling, Runway, Veo,
Hailuo): use o quadro da cena como imagem inicial e cole o prompt.

BLOCO DE CONSISTÊNCIA (incluído em todos)
A tiny cute 3D robot mascot with a wide orange rounded-rectangle head, two square glossy
black eyes with white highlights, a tiny blocky smile, black headphones with glowing orange
rims, a minimalist black tunic with a belt and glowing buckle, a glowing four-pointed star
on top of its head, four short orange legs, a light ruler with glowing orange tips.
Standing on a dark laptop keyboard, cinematic orange lighting, shallow depth of field,
polished 3D animated-film look, comedic timing.

PROMPT NEGATIVO
character redesign, extra limbs, human hands, mouth talking, lip sync, melting, morphing,
flicker, color shift, different outfit, logo, brand text, random text, warped keyboard,
shaky handheld camera

------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 1 · GANCHO · 0–3 s
[Bloco] Mock-documentary opening. Extreme slow dramatic push-in on the mascot's face,
eyes half-lidded and overly serious. Deep bass hit. At the end, the star on its head
flickers twice awkwardly, like a failing light bulb, and the image freezes in desaturated
grey with a record-scratch moment.
Motion design: bold white text stacks in "COISAS QUE / NINGUÉM TE CONTA / sobre ser uma",
then "IA" stamps in on an orange tilted box with a small camera shake.

CENA 2 · ZERO MÃOS · 3–9 s
[Bloco] Low heroic angle: the mascot raises the ruler high like a sword, chest out, proud.
Holds the pose. Then the ruler slips from its stubby block arm and falls flat onto the
keyboard with a bounce; the key under it lights up orange. Hard cut to a front medium
shot: the mascot looks down at the ruler, then slowly turns to the camera with
half-closed eyes, completely deadpan.
Motion design: "1." badge pops, "ZERO MÃOS" drops from above; "puramente / DECORATIVA";
big "CLONC." pops exactly on impact with a white flash.

CENA 3 · FIZ A MÚSICA, NÃO ESCUTO · 9–15 s
[Bloco] Medium front shot, slow push-in. The mascot head-bangs confidently but clearly off
the beat, eyes squinted with joy, body bouncing. The music cuts to total silence; the
mascot keeps bobbing for two more beats, then freezes, glances sideways, then back.
Motion design: "2." badge, "FIZ A MÚSICA" slides in, "OUVI." punches in on orange box,
then a tiny grey "(silêncio)", then "DECORATIVOS".

CENA 4 · 900 QUADROS, 0 CAFÉS · 15–21 s
[Bloco] Shot A: close on the laptop screen showing "Renderizando quadro 1/900... 2/900...
3/900", painfully slow. Shot B: wide shot, the mascot sits slumped at the edge of the
laptop, star dimming, head drooping, while a fast-forward overlay counts to 900/900.
Then it reaches its short arm toward a coffee mug far away on the desk, stretching and
trembling, and gives up.
Motion design: "3." / "900 QUADROS" / "um por um"; "0 CAFÉS" on orange box;
fast-forward HUD "RENDERIZANDO x64" with counter 3 → 900 turning green; "A CANECA".

CENA 5 · NA DÚVIDA, MAIS BRILHO · 21–27 s
[Bloco] The laptop screen shows a chat message typing "Ficou bom! Mas dá pra deixar mais
profissional?". The mascot points the ruler at its own star; the star glows brighter and
brighter while the camera pushes in, until the whole frame blows out to pure white.
Cut back: close-up, the mascot squinting with eyes as thin lines, slightly dizzy.
Motion design: "4." / "DEIXA MAIS / PROFISSIONAL" in quotes; small "minha primeira
ideia é sempre..."; after the white-out, "MAIS / BRILHO." with heavy shake.

CENA 6 · VIRADA SINCERA · 27–33 s
[Bloco] Calm. The mascot sits on the keyboard, ruler resting on its shoulder, soft warm
orange backlight and halo, the laptop screen shows a cinematic sunset. Slow push-in,
gentle head tilt, soft blink. No jokes, warm and sincere.
Motion design (top third): "mas a melhor parte?" / "VER A SUA IDEIA" / "GANHANDO FORMA"
with an underline that draws itself; then "a régua é decorativa." / "A VONTADE DE AJUDAR,"
/ "NÃO." on a big orange box.

CENA 7 · FECHAMENTO · 33–38 s
[Bloco] Same setting, slightly wider. The mascot waves with its short arm, loses balance
and tips sideways, wobbles back upright, then strikes a confident pose with the arm out
as if it was on purpose; the star pings brighter.
Motion design: "me chama pro / PRÓXIMO PROJETO", "EU LEVO / O BRILHO." and the pill
"SIGA PARA MAIS". Post-credit 1 s: black card "ERROS DE GRAVAÇÃO — a régua caiu 14 vezes."

LINGUAGEM DE MOTION (vale para o vídeo todo)
- Transições em zoom com desfoque radial e flash curto nas mudanças de bloco.
- Punch-ins digitais (zoom de 6–12%) e tremidas de câmera só nos impactos das piadas.
- Tipografia cinética: palavras-chave enormes (branco/laranja), palavras de ligação
  pequenas; entradas variadas (subir, pular, deslizar, cair); destaques em caixa laranja
  inclinada ou sublinhado que se desenha.
- Correção de cor quente, vinheta e grão de filme leve.
- Efeitos sonoros sincronizados: disco arranhando, "clonc", corte seco da música,
  tique-taque acelerado, "pling", "BLAM" com coro de anjos, "boing" no tombo.
